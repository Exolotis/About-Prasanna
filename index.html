Venture · Founder · In Build
The gap is where
the pearl is.

Moluscca is a risk-intelligence venture built on one observation: there are markets where cover is mandated or urgently demanded : yet no one holds the data to price it. Moluscca builds the missing scoring layer : transparent insurability scores with a defensible "why" : and delivers it to whoever is currently pricing blind.

The method
Frequency × severity modelled separately. Thin data handled honestly : credibility theory blends real history with class priors, and the weighting is always stated.
The verticals
The vertical rotates : SME cyber, satellite & space, AI-agent liability, gig, climate-adjacent covers. The method does not.
The rule
No black boxes. If an underwriter or regulator couldn't follow the logic of a score, it gets simplified until they can.
