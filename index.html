Risk Analyst, Liabilities and Ongoing

Internship

Actuarial Intern, Actuarial and Climate Risk
  • Parametric products. Solar farm revenue loss, livestock, SRO and climate-sensitive green energy portfolios, single and multi-peril.
  • Climate data. Irradiance, temperature and humidity, modelled into revenue volatility and risk exposure.
